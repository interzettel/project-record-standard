## Purpose and authority

Maintainers and agents should be able to explain what works, what remains, why a percentage changed and which proof is missing. The toolkit supplies a versioned JSON contract, adapters, exact-count calculators, history/delivery records, offline CLI/action/workflow, browser components, eleven agent skills and an additive installer.

`examples/projects.json` selects the sources. `examples/sources/standard/ledger.json` owns scope, state and criteria; registered supplements own changes and delivery observations. Yiğit owns scope and outcome acceptance. Issues own discussion and assignment. README, SVG and Pages project those records. The execution plan owns no duplicate completion state.

## Boundaries and evidence

Individual migrations, live consumer runtimes, private assessments, new credentials, paid services and schedules remain outside this structuring package. Prehnite is a pinned reference. The selected work adds record-boundary proof, evidence/selection guidance, README-only tailoring, a readable JSON inspector and semantic upgrades before broad adoption. Authority guidance, graph views, conventions, physical projections and public discoverability remain later work.

Native Edge 200%/400% zoom was inspected in the released scope; Narrator operation was owner-confirmed without an agent-captured speech transcript or version record. These scoped claims remain historical and do not establish full WCAG conformance or a readiness grade. Read [verification](../VERIFICATION-public.md), [delivery](../standards/DELIVERY.md) and [adoption](adoption.md) for their exact evidence boundaries.
